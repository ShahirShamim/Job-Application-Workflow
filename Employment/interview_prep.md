# 🗣️ PM Behavioral Interview STAR Stories

A structured library of behavioral interview stories mapped using the **Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR)** framework. Useful for PM and Software Engineer interviews.

---

## 🚀 1. [Story Title, e.g., Cloud Migration Project] (Focus Area, e.g., Execution)
* **Question Mapping**: *Tell me about a time you led a complex project.* / *Describe a time you resolved technical debt.*
* **Situation**: [Provide context about the company, marketplace, product size, or problem space.]
* **Task**: [Explain your specific role, responsibilities, and key metrics/goals you needed to deliver.]
* **Action**:
  - [Describe specific action 1 with details on methodologies used.]
  - [Describe specific action 2 with details on engineering or cross-functional alignment.]
  - [Describe specific action 3 detailing how you managed risks or stakeholders.]
* **Result**:
  - [Describe key outcome 1 with **bold metrics** e.g., **40% speedup**.]
  - [Describe key outcome 2 e.g., **+$100k savings**.]
  - [Describe key outcome 3 e.g., **100% compliance**.]

## 🤖 2. [Story Title, e.g., AI Integration Launch] (Focus Area, e.g., Innovation)
* **Question Mapping**: *Tell me about a time you launched an AI/ML product.*
* **Situation**: [Provide context about the problem space and why AI/ML was needed.]
* **Task**: [Explain what model/algorithm you needed to deploy and what the target metrics were.]
* **Action**:
  - [Action 1: Designing data collection pipelines or choosing model architectures.]
  - [Action 2: Establishing guardrails and evaluations to mitigate hallucinations or errors.]
  - [Action 3: User testing and scaling backend infrastructure.]
* **Result**:
  - [Result 1 e.g., **30% reduction in processing time**.]
  - [Result 2 e.g., **80% accuracy**.]
